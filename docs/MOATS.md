# CreatorsAI moats

The test for each moat: (1) AI can't generate it, (2) a funded competitor can't copy it by shipping features, (3) users pay or stay because of it.
Each one gets stronger with every deal logged on the platform, and each one feeds the other two.

## 1. Verified Trust Graph (built in v0.4)
**What:** a two-sided record of who actually delivered and who actually paid, built only from collaborations both parties confirmed (`verifiedCompletion`), with on-time evidence from recorded delivery and payment dates.
**Why it can't be copied:** AI can write a perfect media kit or fake follower screenshots. It can't fabricate a counterparty confirming a completed, paid deal. A competitor starting today begins with zero confirmed history; ours compounds daily.
**Who pays / stays:** brands pay to hire creators with proven on-time records (Growth, Managed Pilots). Creators stay because their record only lives here, and because they can see which brands pay on time. That gives brands a reason to pay on time, which is our leverage over the market.
**Rules that keep it trustworthy:** two-sided confirmation only; delivery dates are never invented; sample sizes always shown; no single opaque score; appeals via data requests.

## 2. Rate & Results Index (built in v0.4)
**What:** real per-unit fees and cost per customer by city × category × deliverable, from completed, paid, tracked deals.
**Why it can't be copied:** it needs (a) agreed fees, (b) recorded payments and (c) tracked outcomes (clicks, redemptions) from the same deals. Agencies have fragments; AI has internet averages; nobody else has outcome-linked local prices for tier-2/3 India.
**Who pays / stays:** brands pay for city-level ranges and cost-per-customer (Growth). Creators get fair-rate guidance free, so they earn more and log more deals, which makes the index more valuable. Privacy: k-anonymity (5+ deals, 3+ creators).

## 3. Local density: city chapters + Creator Pods (Pods built in v0.5; chapters are operational)
**What:** dense, verified creator networks per city, organised offline (meetups, chapters run by local organisers) and online (Creator Pods: teams that take on multi-creator campaigns together, with roles and payment splits).
**Why it can't be copied:** relationships, physical events and trust in a city take months to build and don't transfer by API. Once a city's best creators work in pods here, brands in that city come here, and the reverse holds too.
**Built in v0.5:** pods with roles and % shares, invites by handle, "apply as pod" (every member verified, shares = 100%), pod deals with work assignment, a split ledger and member-confirmed payouts, and per-member credit on the Trust Graph.
**Who pays / stays:** brands pay for one coordinated pod instead of chasing five people (Managed Pilots, and later pod-campaign fees). Organisers and pod leads earn from the platform, so they stay.

## How they compound
Deal Desk (free, single-player) → more confirmed deals → stronger Trust Graph + richer Rate Index → brands prefer and pay → more paid deals → denser cities → better pods → more deals.
