# Founder runbook

**Daily (≈20 min)**
1. `/admin` → check queues.
2. `/admin/waitlist` → review *pending*. Prioritise cohort cities and complete creator profiles. Approve → **Email it** (opens your mail app with the invite pre-filled). Invites only work for that email and expire in 14 days.
3. `/admin/verifications` → open each profile, confirm the exact `CAI-XXXXXX` code is in the bio, sanity-check authenticity. Never verify from screenshots. Reject with a clear reason.
4. `/admin/requests` → acknowledge within 24 h. Resolve deletion by removing profile, handle, social accounts and the Auth user (Firebase console); keep consent/audit records. Write what you did.

**Onboarding traditional members (the switch playbook)**
- Don't pitch "the network". Sit with them and log *one live deal they already have* in the Deal Desk.
- For creators: add their UPI ID in Billing, send one payment reminder from the deal. For businesses: create a tracked link + promo code for their next creator post.
- Ask them to send the agreement link to their client on WhatsApp — that client sees CreatorsAI without signing up.

**Revenue operations**
- Admin overview → "Managed Pilots to start": contact the brand within one business day (email is on the order). Agree objective, budget and timeline in writing; then run the campaign through their Deal Desk so results are tracked.
- Refunds: issue from the Razorpay dashboard, then note it in the audit trail via a data request or your own records. Follow `/refund-policy`.
- Never promise views, sales or virality. Sell transparent coordination and measured results.

**Weekly**: compare waitlist vs. validation targets (50 verified creators · 10 qualified brands · 3 paid pilots). Reach out personally to qualified brands — offer a managed pilot with a curated shortlist.

**Never**: invent activity, publish counts publicly, or let automation suspend anyone.
